# On-Call Roster

If you need to get hold of the Ops team after hours then you can follow these steps

1. Log into Pae Awhina
1. Go to top right hand menu and click on My Workspaces
![image.png](/.attachments/image-16b88385-777d-4284-96c2-5944da7bb13b.png)
1. Click on On-Call Calendar
![image.png](/.attachments/image-e8a2187c-17e6-4e74-af6c-115dda83bc1f.png)
1. Click on the Team Icon
![image.png](/.attachments/image-c7bc0726-0450-4e71-81aa-d5bd3e7fa436.png)
1. Change the Shift filter to After Hours On-Call Shift
![image.png](/.attachments/image-c44a4eb3-1676-4f95-ab3b-b649f00cb597.png)
1. Scroll to the top of the page to see who is on call
![image.png](/.attachments/image-44cf77f0-3912-4654-97cf-d78fb256aa9e.png)
