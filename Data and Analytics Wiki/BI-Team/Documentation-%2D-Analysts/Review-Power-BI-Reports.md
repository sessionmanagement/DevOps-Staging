**Guide for Reviewing Power BI Reports** 

After a year in production it is time to review a Power BI Report.  
 Objectives of the Review
1. Evaluate Report Relevance
1. Evaluate Data Accuracy 
1. Review Report Performance
1. Security and Compliance
1. Report Enhancements/Refinements
1. Stakeholder Communication
1. Training for Stakeholders and Users
   
**Steps to Review Power BI Reports**

**1. Evaluate Report Relevance**
Determine if the reports still align with current business goals and needs and identify any changes in the business that may necessitate updates or new reports.
Review the current business objectives and compare them with the goals of the existing reports and identify any shifts in the business that may require updates to the reports or new reporting needs.

- 	Collect Feedback from Users
- 	Distribute surveys to users who regularly interact with the reports. Ask questions about their satisfaction, any difficulties they face, and suggestions for improvement.
- 	Interviews and Focus Groups: 
- 	Conduct interviews or focus group sessions with key users to gain deeper insights into their experiences with the reports.
- 	Usage Analytics: 
- 	Review Power BI usage analytics to understand how frequently reports are accessed, which pages are viewed most often, and which reports are underutilized.

**2. Evaluate Data Accuracy** 
•	Ensure that the data sources are still valid and that the data is being processed correctly.
•	Check for any discrepancies in the data.  
•	Verify that the data transformations and calculations used in the reports are still accurate and relevant.  
•	Cross reference the data presented in the report with others and source data.

 **3. Assess Report Performance**
•	Analyse the performance of the reports, including load times and responsiveness, by measuring load times.  Identify any visuals that are consistently loading slowly.
•	Review the data models and queries used in the reports to identify opportunities for optimization.
•	Consider implementing improvements such as reducing data granularity, optimizing DAX queries, or using aggregations.
•	Test the reports on different devices (desktop, mobile) to ensure they perform well across all platforms.
•	Ensure that the reports are responsive and provide a good user experience on smaller screens.

 **4. Review Security and Compliance**
•	Review the security settings to ensure data is properly protected.
•	Verify that the reports comply with relevant data governance and privacy policies.
•	Review the security settings and access permissions for each report to ensure that only authorised users have access to sensitive data.
•	Ensure that the reports comply with internal data governance policies, including data classification and handling procedures.
•	Verify compliance with external regulations, especially if the reports handle personal or sensitive data ie Privacy Regulations.

**5.  Report Enhancements/Refinement**
•	Based on user feedback and performance assessments, update the reports to improve clarity, relevance, and usability.
•	Create a detailed list of all changes made to the reports, including updates to data sources, modifications to visuals, and performance optimizations.
•	Include the reasons for each change to provide context.
•	List Changes
•	Collaborate with Scrum Master for back logging of report enhancements and work allocation.

 **6. Stakeholder Communication**
•	Present the findings and changes to stakeholders, including report users, data owners, and management.
•	Highlight the improvements made and explain how they address user feedback and performance issues.
•	Gather continuous feedback on the updated reports to ensure that the changes meet user expectations.
•	Be open to additional suggestions and continue refining the reports as needed.

**7.  Training and Support**
•	Provide training or documentation to help users understand any new features or changes to the reports.
•	Offer ongoing support to address any questions or issues that arise.
