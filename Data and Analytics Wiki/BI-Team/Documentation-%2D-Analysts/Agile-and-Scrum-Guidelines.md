
**Introduction**  
This document outlines the principles, guidelines, and expectations for adopting Agile and Scrum methods in our BI project development. It aims to promote collaboration, adaptability, and continuous improvement.

<details>
<summary><b>The Agile Philosophy</b></summary>

<br>

*What is Agile?*
Agile is a flexible, iterative approach to project management that focuses on customer satisfaction, team collaboration, and smart organization of the development process.

*The 12 Agile Principles* 
1. Customer satisfaction  
2. Welcome change  
3. Deliver frequently  
4. Work together  
5. Support motivated individuals  
6. Communicate face to face  
7. Working software  
8. Maintain a constant pace  
9. Technical excellence  
10. Simplicity  
11. Self-Organizing teams  
12. Reflect and adjust

*Why We Use an Agile Approach* 
Adopting an Agile approach to our BI development allows us to frequently deliver iterations of a larger piece of work to our stakeholders. This allows for regular user testing and feedback to ensure the end product meets the needs of the end user.

<summary><b>The Scrum Framework</b></summary>
 
Scrum is a lightweight framework designed to help teams deliver value by finding adaptive solutions to complex problems. Scrum is one of many frameworks that applies the Agile philosophy.

**The Three Scrum Pillars**  
1. **Transparency**: We make sure that all aspects of the process are visible and understandable to everyone involved. This helps us create a high trust environment.  
2. **Inspection**: We regularly review progress and outcomes to identify any issues or areas for improvement.  
3. **Adaptation**: Regular opportunities to discuss necessary adjustments allow us to continuously improve our processes and the way we work.

<summary><b>The Scrum Methodology for the Emerge BI Team</b></summary>
 <br>

***Roles and Responsibilities***

*Product Owner*
- Defines product Features and their scope  
- Prioritises the Feature backlog based on organizational priorities as decided by the Report Prioritisation Group (RPG).
- Approves completed Features in consultation with relevant managers and stakeholders  
- Works with the Scrum Master to break down Features into manageable tasks and user stories that align with our data delivery goals

*Scrum Master*  
- Facilitates Scrum meetings (except daily stand-ups). 
- Seeks to remove obstacles that hinder the team's progress.  
- Ensures adherence to Agile principles and practices within the BI team.
- Helps manage Feature progress across sprints, considering the impact on data delivery.
- Works with the Product Owner to break down Features into manageable tasks and user stories that align with our data delivery goals.

*Bug Master*  
- Oversees and coordinates bug fixes and urgent issues (Pae Awhina, daily fails)  
- Serves as the primary point of contact for other departments regarding BI-related issues or queries during the sprint  
- Role rotates among team members and lasts for the duration of a single sprint (3 weeks)

*Business Intelligence (BI) Team*  
- The team responsible for delivering quality, working, and up-to-date Features that support Emerge Aotearoa's data and reporting needs  
- Creates and maintains their own DevOps boards  
- Estimates and tracks progress (hours) on tasks and user stories within Features

***Events***

*Sprints*  
A sprint is a three-week period within which we aim to deliver iterations of larger project to the stakeholder, allowing for continuous user testing and feedback. During the sprint we perform daily stand-ups, planning, review, and retrospective meetings. At the start of each sprint, a sprint backlog is created from the product backlog. The sprint backlog contains all the work the team aims to complete within the sprint. Features may span multiple sprints, but user stories should be deliverable within a single sprint.

*Daily Stand-up*
Daily stand-ups are a way to share progress, discuss blockers, and plan for the day's work. This includes discussion on any 'fixes' that need to be performed and any urgent work that affects data delivery. This stand-up is led by the Bug Master.

*Sprint Planning Meeting*  
The sprint planning meeting happens at the start of each sprint. During this meeting the product backlog is reviewed and a sprint backlog is created based on product prioritisation. Outcomes of the meeting are; Plan the upcoming sprint, define sprint goals, and select tasks and user stories from Features for development, considering their impact on our data services.

*Mid-Sprint Review Meeting*  
This meeting aims at assessing sprint progress, Feature status, and re-aligning sprint goals if necessary and appropriate. It is held half way through every sprint.

*Sprint Review Meeting* 
These meetings are held at the end of every sprint. During the meeting we focus on demonstrating completed work to other members of the BI Team. If beneficial, we invite stakeholders to these demonstrations, and gather feedback on the delivered User Stories from that sprint.

*Sprint Retrospective*
Sprint Retrospective meetings are held once every three sprints. They are aimed at reflecting on the past sprints, identify areas for improvement in our processes, and plan actions on how to improve on delivering according to the 12 agile principles. 

*Education and Training*  
The first Monday morning and last Friday evening of each sprint are used for educational tasks. This is the time to catch up on training and development.
