When a report is decommissioned or replaced, the following steps should be taken to ensure the report is taken out of action but can be restored if needed later.

1. Delete the report from the Te Pataka app and republish
2. Download a copy of the report and dataset and save into our archive folder in the new reporting structure
3. Delete those files from Te Pataka or Production.
4. Remove the report from the Reporting Catalogue (the sharepoint list will still have it in the history if needed)
5. Remove the files from the latest GIT.
