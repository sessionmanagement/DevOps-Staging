Business Intelligence Resources Hub
===================================

Overview
--------

This central repository houses technical guides, scripts, validation tools, and best practices for the BI team. It serves as a knowledge base for various data domains and analytical processes across our organization.

[[_TOSP_]]

Purpose
-------

*   Centralize technical knowledge and resources
*   Document standard procedures and methodologies
*   Share reusable components and solutions
*   Maintain consistency across BI deliverables
*   Accelerate onboarding and knowledge transfer

Adding Content
--------------

To contribute, please follow the standard page template and categorize your content appropriately. Contact the BI Team Lead for questions about organization or structure.

Maintenance
-----------

Pages should be reviewed quarterly to ensure continued relevance and accuracy. Note the last-updated date on all technical content.
